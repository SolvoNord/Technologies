Close-up of the cable management, softly lit neutral white technical workspace, natural daylight, premium understated architectural photography aesthetic, highly realistic materials and physically plausible RJ45 connectors. Rack and curved cable runs mostly occupy the right two thirds; on the left allow quieter shadowed dark negative space for white website typography that will be added separately. Calm, clean, professional, residential and small business scale, no sci-fi, no neon, no datacenter corridors. The brand green is #015f41; no other bright accent colors. No people, no legible text, no words, no logo, no watermark, no decorative interface, no fake project labels.
